Its broad filled arms, legs, small paddle hands and rounded feet are also ink-dark. No hair, clothing, accessories, mouth, nose or eyebrows. The featureless body is about four head-heights tall.

3–7s: The right hand makes contact with the ring and closes around one side; the ring stays a single object.
